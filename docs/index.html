What this search can — and cannot — tell you
Semantic similarity can point to related language. It cannot establish intent, historical truth, or whether a question has an answer. Even the highest-ranked passage may be irrelevant. The provisional 0.4 threshold is not a calibrated confidence score. Low scores do not prove that something is absent.
The “Waiting at home” example deliberately preserves a known retrieval failure. Read the alternative matches rather than trusting the first.
Model: Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2, the quantized ONNX conversion of sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (Apache-2.0). English prototype. Questions remain in browser memory. The hosting provider receives page requests; model hosts receive file-download requests. Browser cache eviction can require another download. Offline reload is not guaranteed.
